Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Shira Gui Photography operates shiraguiphotography.com and a private client portal for managing photo galleries and contracts. This policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your information.

Information I Collect

I collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, and any details submitted through the contact form or client portal. I also store photos taken during photography sessions securely on Google Drive.

How I Use Your Information

I use your information to deliver photography services, share your photo gallery with you, send contracts for signature, and communicate about your session. I do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Photo Storage

Your photos are stored on Google Drive and accessed through your private gallery link. Gallery access requires a unique token and, for downloads, a download code provided by me.

Cookies

The client portal uses cookies to maintain your session and remember that you have unlocked your gallery downloads. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Data Retention

Your photos and contract information are retained for the duration of our relationship. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting me.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact me at shiraxgui@gmail.com.
